About Me

People > Products > Impact.

Hi! My name is Prisha. I'm currently pursuing my Master's in AI at Northeastern, and graduated with honors with a B.S. in Business Economics and a minor in Cognitive Science from Hofstra.

My path to product management was not linear, but I like to think that is what sets me apart. I started by studying how people think and make decisions, then layered in how markets work, what drives consumer behavior, and how to weigh trade-offs. I'm focused on human-centered technology, building products that are thoughtful, responsible, and designed with real people in mind.

Three Lenses I Bring To Product

Cognitive Science

Understanding How People Think

Why do users make the choices they do? What mental models are they working with? I think about behavior before I think about features.

Business Economics

Incentives, Trade-offs, Consumer Behavior

Every product decision has a cost. I am trained to identify what drives behavior at scale and where the real trade-offs lie.

Artificial Intelligence

A Tool, Not A Box

I have built AI systems and studied how they land in the real world. I care about what they can do for users, who they affect, and whether they are being built responsibly.
